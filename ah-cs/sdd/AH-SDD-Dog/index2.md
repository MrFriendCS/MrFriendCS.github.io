# AH SDD - A Dog's Life Part 2


## Introduction

The virtual dogs are a run away success.
Vatersay Virtual Pet Service (VVPS) now is going to expand the range of dogs.
There will be now be a variant that models an assistance dog, and another that models a show dog.


### Characteristics

VVPS has determined that the following characteristics are important:


#### Assistance Dog

* Each dog has a name and an age.
* On a dog's birthday, it will age 1 human year.
* A dog can eat anything, and will enjoy it.


#### Show Dog

* Each dog has a name and an age.
* On a dog's birthday, it will age 1 human year.
* A dog can eat anything, and will enjoy it.
