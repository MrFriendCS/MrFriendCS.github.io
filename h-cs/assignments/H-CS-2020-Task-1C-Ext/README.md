### Questions

***1d(i)*** The customer who had the most expensive car repair is to be sent a £50 voucher off their next repair.  Display the full name, address and car registration of this customer.  (**4 marks**)

***1d(ii)*** The company wish to find the make and model of the cars that are the cheapest to repair.  Find the cost of the cheapest repair and then display the car make and model of any car whose repair cost less than £20 more than the cheapest repair.  (**4 marks**)

***1d(iii)*** Find the number of jobs completed by each garage.  Display the name of any garages that have less than 15 jobs in the database.  (**4 marks**)

***1d(iv)*** Find the number of customers each garage has carried out repairs for and display the largest number of customers found.  (**4 marks**)
